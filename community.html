Selected Work

  1. 01

    NetEase Blizzard China Gaming Content Creator

    Creator Partnership · Digital Media / Gaming Content Creator · 2024–Present

    Audience
    20K+ Douyin followers
    Platforms
    Douyin and Bilibili, short-form video and longer localised talks

    I create gaming content for Chinese audiences while using each release as an ongoing read on the Chinese game market and player response.

    The work covers Hearthstone and broader gaming content, cosplay, livestream formats, and short-form video. I usually run the whole production loop myself: choosing the topic, recording, editing, and publishing.

    Because the audience is primarily Chinese players, every release doubles as a way to stay close to that market: which games and topics are drawing attention, what players actually care about, which formats hold up, and how the same piece lands differently from one platform to another. Views, saves, comments, and the discussion underneath a post all feed into what I make next.

    Localisation is the other half of the work. I translate and adapt game-design and developer talks for Bilibili, so Chinese-speaking audiences can reach developer perspectives and design knowledge that would otherwise stay behind a language barrier.

  2. 02

    Schoolive Education Community & Learning Platform

    Schoolive Interactive Ltd. · Co-founder / Creative Director · 2022–Present

    I co-founded Schoolive and shape how psychology, game design, and interactive-media resources are organised for Chinese-speaking learners.

    Schoolive is a free online education platform providing psychology, game design and interactive media resources for Chinese-speaking students. I co-founded it and I am responsible for its creative direction.

    The work is content architecture rather than platform administration: how courses are organised, what a learning path looks like from a beginner’s first session through to a later one, and which material should be published, updated, or reorganised because its current shape is not working.

    Learner feedback drives most of those decisions. When people stall in the same place, that is usually a sequencing problem rather than a content gap, and the fix is structural. How material is ordered is often as important as the material itself.

    The same platform also carries an educational game project whose creative direction I lead. That work sits under Game Design.

  3. 03

    HEYBOX Rainbow Six Wiki & Player Community

    HEYBOX / Rainbow Six Siege community · Community & Wiki Contributor

    I worked on turning dense, fast-changing Rainbow Six Siege knowledge into a structure players could search, scan, and keep current.

    Rainbow Six Siege carries a large amount of detail that matters in play: operators, gadgets, map geometry, counters, and the way all of it shifts with each balance change. Players do not arrive wanting to read a page. They arrive with a specific question, usually mid-frustration.

    So the work was information design for a live game. I looked at what players were trying to find and could not, worked out how an entry should be structured to answer that quickly, and kept the structure consistent from entry to entry, so the second page someone reads behaves like the first.

    Maintenance is the other half. Entries have to be updated as the game changes underneath them, or the structure stops being worth trusting. Consistency across entries is a system decision, and deciding what to leave out is most of the work.
