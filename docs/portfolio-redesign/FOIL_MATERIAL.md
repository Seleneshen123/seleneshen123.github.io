# Tarot card — neutral base and holographic foil

**Inspection page:** `preview/c2/lab/foil.html` (local: http://localhost:4191/preview/c2/lab/foil.html)

## The correction
The rainbow gradient in the V2 artwork's arched central panel was a baked *simulation* of holographic foil, not the printed colour; the printed base is neutral grey. The 3D card therefore needs (a) a base texture with that panel restored to grey and (b) a real, angle-dependent foil material.

## Derived textures (non-destructive)
Generated by `prototypes/tools/derive-tarot.mjs` from `tarot-card-design-v2-tight-758.webp` (read-only), written to `preview/c2/textures/`. The original artwork files are never modified.

**Grey reference:** the tint is sampled from the panel of the earlier `tarot-card-design-tight-755.webp` (which has a neutral panel) under the same mask, then clamped to ±3.5 % chroma so it stays a neutral warm grey: RGB multipliers 1.033 / 0.999 / 0.965. Mean panel colour: before (93, 104, 90) green-cast → after (104, 100, 97). The earlier artwork is used **only** for this tint; every V2 design detail is kept.

No new artwork details were invented. No normal map was created (the printed card is flat; the bevelled gilded edge provides the physical relief).

## Material (`prototypes/src/c2/tarot-engine-c2.js`)
`MeshPhysicalMaterial` with the neutral base as `map`, the surface map for roughness/metalness, clearcoat 0.55 (roughness 0.16), plus two foil layers:

1. **Physically based thin film** — `iridescence` 0.4, `iridescenceMap` = foil R, `iridescenceThicknessMap` = foil G, `iridescenceIOR` 1.5, thickness 240–520 nm (`iridescenceThicknessMinimum/Maximum`, three r187). Gives the soft oil-film colour shift at grazing angles.
2. **Diffraction term (custom, injected before `opaque_fragment`)** — standard iridescence alone reads as a soap film, not a premium holo card, so the card's *own specular light* (direct + indirect) is dispersed into a spectrum along a diagonal grating fixed in card space. The phase combines position across the card (coherent diagonal bands), the reflection vector's projection on the grating (the bands travel as the card or camera moves) and a slight thickness waver. The spectrum **redistributes** the reflected light around its own luminance rather than adding light, so colour appears only where the card is actually catching light, never as a flat, permanent or emissive rainbow. Saturation 0.6; strength scaled by the foil map, so gold keeps its metallic identity and the text panel stays almost matte.

Bloom is unchanged (threshold 0.9) so the foil cannot flare. The previous "specular sweep" emissive band is disabled for this card.

## Verifying
The inspection page shows: 1 · original V2 · 2 · derived neutral base · the panel mask · the foil map; an interactive card (drag or sliders: yaw ±40°, pitch ±25°, light angle, foil strength) with **Foil off / Thin film / Film + spectrum**, three lighting set-ups (the homepage spotlight, studio, raking) and a switch between the neutral base and the baked V2 for direct comparison; and **3 · dynamic foil at −30°, −15°, 0°, +15°, +30°** rendered live under the homepage spotlight.

Rotation stays front-facing; no card back is shown or integrated.
