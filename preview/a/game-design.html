[ B ] Selected games

Playable work.

G-01 Lunch Hours Game Art · Game Design · Gameplay Design A lunchtime obstacle course built around the panic of beating the cafeteria queue. Dialogue boxes block the path, so a social interruption becomes speed and space pressure.
G-02 Cena Floris Game Design · Narrative / Worldbuilding · Gameplay · Art A cooking-magic game where the recipe only becomes visible when you ask for it, and every second you look burns more of it away. Information itself is the resource.
G-03 COWorker Game Design · Gameplay · Art An experimental feminist game about productivity, bodily control, and the fact that the player is also trapped inside the system. 18+ content gate on itch.io
G-04 Dueling Duet Art · Character Design · Animation Two duellists from opposite mythologies and two instruments rebuilt as living monsters. Characters, instrument-monsters, Gothic letterforms and the hand-animated music-box opening.
