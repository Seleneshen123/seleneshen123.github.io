A — Cinematic Futurism

Reference Arknights: EndfieldType Archivo (expanded) + IBM Plex Mono

A cold, light "frontier dossier": expanded technical display type, a strict 12-column grid, hairline frames and spec tables. The card is a specimen suspended in a pale clean-room volume, with fine dark dust in a lateral current and measured orbital rings.

B — Ethereal Fantasy

Reference Wuthering Waves · Cardcaptor SakuraType Cormorant + Jost

A night-tide world that persists behind every page. The card is summoned — it rises with a slow in-plane turn inside a helix of luminous motes; scrolling carries the camera up through the particles. Light serif display, staggered layouts and echoing hairline frames.

C — Cinematic Minimalism

Reference Death Stranding 2 · editorial game sitesType Instrument Serif + Instrument Sans

Near-black and patient. The card stands alone under a single overhead light with dust only inside the beam; work is presented as 2.39:1 film stills with credit typography, four-corner navigation and fade-to-black cuts between pages.
