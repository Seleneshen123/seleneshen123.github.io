Case Study

3D Astronomy Education Game Playtesting

Role
Research Assistant
Period
July 2022–September 2022
Context
Learning, Representations, & Technology Lab · UW–Madison
OverviewObservation FocusFeedback SynthesisRelevance

Overview

This research experience focused on game-based STEM learning and representational competencies in a 3D astronomy education game.

Observation Focus

I observed playtesting sessions and documented learner navigation, verbal reasoning, confusion points, and interactions with astronomy-related visual representations.

Feedback Synthesis

  • Organized participant feedback to support qualitative review of learners’ sense-making processes.
  • Synthesized observation notes and transcripts into playtest summaries.
  • Helped identify usability barriers, conceptual misunderstandings, and possible instructional design improvements.

Relevance

The experience connected behavioral observation with iterative game and learning-experience design. No participant counts or outcomes are stated here beyond the documented responsibilities.
