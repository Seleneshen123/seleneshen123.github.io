Case Study

Play-based Chinese Character Learning Activity

Role
Teacher & Learning Activity Designer
Period
August 2024–August 2025
Context
If Montessori School · Houston, Texas
OverviewObservation BasisActivity DesignPrivacyDesign Relevance

Overview

This activity grew from work with preschool-aged children in a Montessori classroom and from observations of attention, learning engagement, social interaction, and responses to hands-on learning materials.

Observation Basis

The design drew on observed patterns in children’s visual attention, symbolic association, and interaction with learning materials.

Activity Design

I developed a play-based Chinese character learning activity and used developmentally appropriate, Montessori-informed strategies to support autonomy, language development, self-directed exploration, and classroom participation.

Privacy

No children’s names, identifiable photographs, or sensitive classroom records are included in this public portfolio.

Design Relevance

The project connects child development, observation, symbolic learning, and hands-on interaction design.
