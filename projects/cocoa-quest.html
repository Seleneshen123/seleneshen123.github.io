Overview

Cocoa Quest explores cocoa-related materials, sustainable packaging, chocolate production, and the social and environmental issues connected to cocoa supply chains.

The project combines biomaterial experimentation, packaging and structural design, food-related education, and sustainability with interactive educational game concepts. It has two stages so far: a first prototype built around packaging and an educational kit, and a later direction towards a tabletop game.

Prototype 1 — Initial Prototype

The first prototype is a packaging and educational-kit prototype. Its packaging uses a gelatin-based biomaterial composite incorporating components such as glycerol, kaolin clay, coconut coir, and cacao nibs. An inner beeswax coating has also been explored for moisture protection.

Note

The composite and the beeswax coating are material experiments. No performance or sustainability results are claimed for them here.

Tabletop game direction

The team’s design direction has since moved towards educational tabletop gameplay. The topics it is being built to address:

  • how chocolate is produced
  • the cocoa supply chain
  • child labor and ethical production
  • environmental impacts
  • material reuse

This is a direction in development, separate from Prototype 1. The game is not finished, and the topics above are its intended scope rather than completed features.
