Generate / Deeplight Games

Concept design and 2D art for a client-commissioned 3D action roguelike: the game’s visual direction, its bosses and characters, and the environments they are fought in.

  • 2D

Overview

The project is a 3D action roguelike commissioned by a client and built by a multidisciplinary team. I work on how it looks, and on keeping the way it looks tied to how it plays.

My Contribution

  • The game’s overall visual direction, developed from the client’s requirements
  • Concept development
  • Boss and character design
  • Boss-related environments and maps
  • 2D art and visual assets
  • Aligning visual design with gameplay requirements

The game itself is built by the whole team. My part is concept design and 2D art, not the development of the game as a whole.
