Overview

Schoolive Interactive is a Moodle-based educational platform providing psychology, game design, and interactive media learning resources for Chinese-speaking students.

Content Design

I developed and organized course content with attention to instructional sequencing, learner accessibility, and clarity of psychological and design-related concepts.

Educational Game Direction

I directed development of an educational game project integrating character design, narrative design, and gameplay mechanics to support learner engagement and interactive knowledge acquisition.

My Role

  • Co-founded the platform and helped define its content direction.
  • Organized course materials and learning sequences.
  • Led the creative direction of an educational game project.

Next Steps

Future updates can include platform captures, course-flow examples, and selected game-development materials once they are prepared for public presentation.
